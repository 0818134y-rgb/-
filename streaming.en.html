Streaming & Video Guidelines

Wachapa welcomes let's plays, live streams and videos. Within the scope below, you don't need to contact us or ask for permission.

You're welcome to

  • Live stream, make let's plays, clips and shorts on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, X and any other platform
  • Monetize them (ad revenue, memberships, tips, Super Chats and so on) — whether you're an individual, a group, a VTuber or with an agency
  • Play the in-game music and sound effects on your stream or video (all of it was made for Wachapa, and we will never claim rights to it)
  • Use game screenshots, character art and the logo in your thumbnails and stream overlays
  • Show the room code on screen and play together with your viewers

Please don't

  • Present Wachapa as a game you made
  • Modify, redistribute or sell the game files themselves
  • Turn the character art into merchandise or products other than for introducing Wachapa

Optional, but appreciated

  • Mention "Wachapa https://wachapa.com/" in your description
  • Use the hashtag #wachapa on X so we can come and watch

Tips for playing with viewers

  • Up to 8 players can be in one room. The streamer should be the host (the one who creates the room)
  • If a troll joins, you can tap "Remove" in the lobby. Showing the room code just before you start is safest
  • Talking games (Word Wolf, Forbidden Words, Line It Up) are best with a voice call such as Discord. Draw & Pass and Chaos Arena work without a call
  • On a PC browser, you can capture the window directly in your streaming software

For advertising campaigns or use on TV, in magazines and so on, please contact us.
