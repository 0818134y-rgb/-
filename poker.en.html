Players: 2–8

Play Poker together — everyone joins from their own phone or PC. No download, no account.

How to play

Pay a 10-chip ante, get 5 cards, and swap as many as you like once. Then everyone decides at the same time to "Call (+10)" or "Fold". The best hand among those who call takes the whole pot.
