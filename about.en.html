About This Site

"Wachapa" is a collection of party games to play when you get together with friends or family — or over a voice call. Chaos Arena, Line It Up, Word Wolf, Forbidden Words, Who's the Spy?, Mind Sync, 20 Questions, Who's Most Likely? and Draw & Pass: all 9 are free to play.

Supported devices

  • iPhone / iPad (Safari — add it to your Home Screen to use it like an app)
  • Android (browser or the Android app)
  • PC (Edge, Chrome and other browsers, or the PC version's launcher)

Any combination of devices can play in the same room using a room code.

How it works

This site doesn't use the operator's own servers. The game runs on the device of whoever created the room, and players' devices talk to each other directly (a PeerJS signaling server is used only to find each other). The names and answers you enter are never sent to the operator.

Why it's free

Ads are shown in the lobby, on result screens and during breaks between games. The ad revenue keeps the site running.
